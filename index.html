How to play

Five moves. That's the whole manual.

Raise & lower

Your right hand height sets the pitch. Notes snap to the scale.

Pinch

Touch thumb + index to pluck a bell note.

Open & close

Your left hand opens the filter and the volume.

Fist

Close a hand into a fist for a drum hit.

Spread

Move hands apart for a bigger room reverb.
